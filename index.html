13 Saal Mubarak

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ فِي الْعُمْرِ وَالرِّزْقِ وَالْأَهْلِ

Ya Allah! Umar, rizq aur ghar walon mein barkat ata farma.

✦

Aaj ke din dil se sirf shukar aur dua hai. Allah tala aap ki umar mein barkat de, sehat-e-kamila ata farmaye aur rizq-e-halal naseeb kare. Aap ke har aane wale saal ko khair, sukoon aur khushi se bhara hua banaye, aur dunya-o-aakhirat dono mein kaamyaabi ata farmaye. Ameen. 🌙

Har khushi Allah ki taraf se hai — Alhamdulillah.
